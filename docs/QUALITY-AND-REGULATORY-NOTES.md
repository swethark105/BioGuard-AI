# Quality and regulatory framing

This independent portfolio project is not affiliated with any commercial organization and does not reproduce proprietary technologies or confidential information. Sample IDs, fragment lengths, thresholds and results are fictional.

ISO 9001:2015 clause references in requirements.json are **illustrative mappings** for discussion of controlled operations, documented information, monitoring, and release controls. They are not an audit or claim of ISO 9001 certification. ISO 13485, FDA software validation guidance and 21 CFR Part 11 may be relevant in other contexts but applicability must be determined for an actual product and intended use; this demo does not claim to implement them.

The in-memory audit event list is an educational example only: it is not durable, tamper-evident, access-controlled or Part 11 compliant. The sample PII test checks selected field names, not a comprehensive privacy assessment. Requirements traceability maps test IDs to scenarios, not validated regulatory requirements.

Risk-based priorities: REQ-002, REQ-003, REQ-004 and REQ-005 are High; REQ-001 is Medium. Release readiness would require human assessment of any failed High-risk scenario.
